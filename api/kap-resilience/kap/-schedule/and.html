kap-resilience/kap/Schedule/and

and

infix fun and(other: Schedule<A>): Schedule<A>

Multiplication of the schedule semiring: both schedules must agree to continue. Delays merge with max (the slower schedule sets the pace — a policy is as patient as its most patient constraint). If either says Decision.Done the result is Decision.Done (a Decision.Done is the delay +∞, so max with it is +∞).

Laws: associative, commutative, identity Schedule.immediate(), annihilator Schedule.never(), and distributive over or (a and (b or c) == (a and b) or (a and c)). Property-tested in ScheduleLawsTest.

// At most 5 retries, each waiting at least 100 ms, growing ×2
Schedule.times<Throwable>(5) and Schedule.exponential(100.milliseconds)
