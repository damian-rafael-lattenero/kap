withPair

fun <A, B> Kap<A>.withPair(fa: Kap<B>): Kap<Pair<A, B>>

Runs this computation and fa in parallel and pairs their results.

Barrier-aware: when this computation is a phase barrier (created by then, thenPair or asPhaseBarrier), fa's launch is gated until the barrier completes, and the pairing computation becomes a barrier gated by the same signal — preserving the phase semantics of the surrounding chain.

This is the generic form of with: with(fa) == withPair(fa).map { (f, a) -> f(a) }. Prefer with on curried-function spines; use withPair when you want the raw pair without a curried function.
